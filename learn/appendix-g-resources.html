🗓️ Daily Practice Routine (15 minutes)

MinuteActivity
0–3Pick a real task from your day
3–8Write a prompt using the R-T-C-I-E-F framework
8–12Run it; critique the output against the cheat sheet
12–15Improve one thing; log the change in your prompt journal

Weekly: take one prompt you use often and build a 10-case mini eval for it. Monthly: re-take the Final Exam (Appendix D) and one new capstone level.
